# 🚀 HireLens AI

## 👋 Your Personal AI Recruiter

### See Your Resume Through a Recruiter's Eyes.

HireLens AI is not just another AI Resume Analyzer.

It simulates how recruiters evaluate resumes, predicts interview chances, detects hidden recruiter red flags, visualizes ATS performance, and provides AI-powered career insights to help candidates improve their resumes and increase their chances of getting hired.

---

## 🎯 Vision

HireLens AI is designed to become an AI-powered virtual recruiter rather than just a resume analyzer.

The goal is to help users understand:

- How recruiters review resumes
- Their interview probability
- Hidden weaknesses in their resume
- ATS compatibility
- Practical suggestions to improve their chances of getting hired
